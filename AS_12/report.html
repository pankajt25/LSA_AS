🛡️ Enterprise Retention & Lifecycle Policies

Backup Lifecycle & Storage Management Standards:

  • Grandfather-Father-Son (GFS) Rotation: Maintain daily differentials for 7 days (Son), weekly rollups for 4 weeks (Father), and monthly snapshots for 12 months (Grandfather).
  • Companion Artifact Hygiene: Always prune companion metadata (.sha256, .md5, .log) in lockstep with parent archive deletion to prevent inode leakage.
  • Safety Minimum Safeguard: Never allow cleanup routines to prune all archives. Enforce KEEP_MIN >= 1 to safeguard against catastrophic zero-backup scenarios during extended outages.
  • Scheduled Automation: Schedule pruning immediately after successful backup creation via cron: 0 3 * * * /usr/local/bin/old_backup_cleaner.sh -d 7 --delete.
